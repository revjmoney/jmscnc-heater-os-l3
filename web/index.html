UTILITY PERIODS

Daily mining schedule

One Off time and one On time, every day. Off pauses ASIC work while CGMiner keeps temperature and fan control running. Edit the times when your summer or winter utility period changes.

Times follow the selected timezone and daylight saving time. Overnight windows are supported. Fan operation and actual power reduction during a scheduled pause still need physical verification.

DEVELOPER SUPPORT

Keep JMSCNC Heater OS going

This firmware is free and open source. If you want to chip in, it can donate a small, clearly shown slice of each hour to the developer's pool, then switch right back to your own pools. It is on at 1% by default, which is about 36 seconds of every hour. Turn it off any time with one click, or set anything from 0% to 5%.

—

How it works and where it goes

  • During the slice, the developer pool is added to CGMiner's live pool list and mined to; when the slice ends it switches back and the pool is removed.
  • Your saved pools are never modified. A reboot or mining restart always comes back to only your own pools.
  • There is no fallback donation pool. If it is unreachable, your own pools keep mining.
  • The slice supports the JMSCNC developer. No personal account details or on-chain address are shown here.

OWNER-SIGNED UPDATES

Update firmware in your browser

Upload an L3 Lab update signed with your owner key. The dashboard checks the signature, model, image CRCs, available RAM and rootfs capacity, then creates a recovery backup.

Initial installation on stock 2019 firmware needs a verified bootstrap or recovery method because Bitmain's uploader accepts Bitmain-signed packages. This updater is available after L3 Lab is installed. The 0.1.2 installation passed NAND readback verification and booted successfully; each new update is verified again before reboot.
